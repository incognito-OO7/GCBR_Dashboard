Priority Intelligence Requirement

Will Taiwan's three major ABF substrate makers still be running at or near full capacity as of 6 October 2026, with ABF utilisation at or above 90% and combined September revenue at or above August's NT$27.9 billion, as per TWSE filings and company disclosures?

Full wording in "Resolution & fallback rule" below.

EEI → Evidence → Source drill-down
click an EEI, then an evidence item
Resolution criteria

YES if both hold: Unimicron's ABF utilisation ≥90% AND combined September revenue (Unimicron + Nan Ya PCB + Kinsus) ≥ NT$27.9bn. If no Q3 utilisation update is published by 6 Oct, that half is marked UNCONFIRMED-PERSISTING (last known: above 90%, stated 29 Jul 2026) rather than resolved YES, pending Unimicron's 27 Oct earnings call. NO if either condition clearly fails. UNCERTAIN if too little has published to judge either side.

Fallback rule

Revenue side: if Unimicron's September filing hasn't published by 6 Oct, the revenue condition is checked on Nan Ya PCB + Kinsus only, against their combined August figure of NT$10.16bn. Utilisation side: no separate fallback source needed — it's built into the resolution criteria itself (UNCONFIRMED-PERSISTING). If both sides are unresolved, the PIR resolves UNCERTAIN outright.
